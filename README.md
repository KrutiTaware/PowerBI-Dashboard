# Diwali Insights Dashboard

This interactive Power BI dashboard provides detailed insights into sales and customer demographics during the Diwali season. The goal of the dashboard is to analyze buying behavior, customer segmentation, and product performance to assist in data-driven decision-making for marketing and inventory strategies.

**Key Metrics:**
Total Revenue Generated: ₹106.50M
Unique Customers: 11.25K
Total Orders Placed: 28K
Occupation Types: 15
Product Categories in Demand: 18

The Diwali Insights Dashboard highlights strong sales performance driven primarily by the 26-35 age group and female customers. With a diverse range of product categories in demand and significant contributions from key states, the dashboard offers valuable insights to optimize marketing efforts and inventory management for future festive seasons. The impressive revenue exceeding sales targets underscores the success of this campaign and provides a solid foundation for strategic growth.
